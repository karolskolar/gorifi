> (RD-FL-2, RD-FL-6, RD-FO-4, RD-GX-1, RD-GX-3).** A text-selection drag that *starts* inside
> `.m-body` and *releases* over the scrim **closes the modal**. Reproduced: `mousedown` on a

> half-filled form by dragging to select text is real data loss. Fix needs a UC-DS-010 amendment:
> additionally require the *mousedown* to have originated on the scrim
